The sun and moon are placed in the sky, but they do not yet know their proper courses.

## Ordering the Cosmos

Light exists but lacks purpose. The celestial bodies await their assignment.

## Significance

Even cosmic forces require the gods' ordering hand. Nature does not organize itself.
