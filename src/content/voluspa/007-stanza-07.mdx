The gods meet at Iðavöllr, building shrines and forges, creating treasures. They play games and are joyful.

## The First Peace

This golden age represents the world before conflict—before the first war, before death.

## Significance

Happiness is remembered, not hoped for. The Norse golden age is in the past.
