The great catalogue of dwarf names begins with Móðsognir, mightiest of all, and Durinn second.

## The Dvergatal

This list of dwarf names (Dvergatal) may be a later addition, but it preserves dwarf lore.

## Significance

Names carry power. To name the dwarves is to establish their place in the cosmic order.
