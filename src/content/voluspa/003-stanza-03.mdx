Before creation there was nothing—no earth, no sky, no sea. Only Ginnungagap, the yawning void.

## Ginnungagap

The primordial emptiness from which everything emerged. Neither hot nor cold, neither light nor dark.

## Significance

This "creation from nothing" parallels other mythologies while remaining distinctly Norse.
