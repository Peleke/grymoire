The gods again convene to discuss who should create the race of dwarves from Brimir's blood and Bláinn's limbs.

## The Dwarf Makers

Dwarves emerge from primordial matter, destined to be the greatest smiths and craftsmen.

## Significance

Even the creation of lesser beings requires divine council and deliberation.
