The gods played, rejoiced, and had gold in abundance—until three giant maidens came from Jötunheim.

## The End of Innocence

The arrival of the giant women marks the transition from golden age to the age of conflict.

## Significance

Paradise is disrupted not by serpents but by the intrusion of the outside world.
