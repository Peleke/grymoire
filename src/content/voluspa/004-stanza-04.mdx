Odin, Vili, and Vé—the sons of Bur—raise the earth from the primordial void, creating Midgard.

## The First Act

The gods' first great deed: shaping habitable land from chaos. This establishes their role as creators and orderers.

## Significance

Creation through action and will, not through mere word. The Norse gods work.
