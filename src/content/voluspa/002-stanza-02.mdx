The völva reveals her ancient origin—she remembers the primordial giants and the nine worlds of Norse cosmology.

## The Nine Worlds

The poem references Yggdrasil's roots reaching to nine worlds, establishing the cosmic structure that will later be destroyed.

## Significance

The seeress's memory extends to before creation, lending her prophecy absolute authority.
