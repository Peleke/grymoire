The Völuspá opens with the völva (seeress) demanding silence from all creatures—gods, humans, and the children of Heimdall.

## The Opening

She announces that she will speak of ancient things, the oldest memories she can recall. This sets the cosmic scope.

## Significance

The poem's authority comes from this opening: a prophet speaking to Odin himself, commanding even the gods to listen.
