Ræið continues Elder Futhark's Raidho—the journey, the ride, movement with purpose. For Vikings, travel was life itself.

## Meaning

Journey, riding, and purposeful movement. The Vikings were travelers par excellence—raiders, traders, and settlers who crossed oceans.

## In the Viking Age

Found on runestones commemorating those who died far from home, on voyages to distant lands.
