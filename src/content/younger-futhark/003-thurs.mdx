Þurs retains the Elder Futhark meaning of "giant" (þurs/thurs). The giants (jötnar) represented chaos, the forces that existed before and beyond the gods' ordered cosmos.

## Meaning

Giants, chaos, and dangerous power. The þursar were not simply enemies—they were the necessary Other against which order defines itself.

## In the Viking Age

Giants appear throughout Norse mythology as adversaries, lovers, and ancestors of the gods themselves. Thor's mother was a giantess.
