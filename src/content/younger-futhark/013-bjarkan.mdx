Bjarkan is the birch tree—first to leaf in spring, symbol of new beginnings. It continues Elder Futhark's Berkano.

## Meaning

New growth, renewal, resilience, and the beauty that emerges from harsh conditions. The birch thrives where other trees cannot.

## In the Viking Age

The rune poems call birch "the greenest of branches"—celebrating its early spring leaves as promise of summer's return.
