Maðr means "man" or "human"—continuing Elder Futhark's Mannaz. It represents humanity in all its complexity.

## Meaning

Humanity, mortality, community, and the bittersweet nature of human existence. We are "dust's increase"—rising from earth, returning to it.

## In the Viking Age

The rune poems speak of man as both "joy to his kinsmen" and destined to fail. Clear-eyed acceptance of mortality.
