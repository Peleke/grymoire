Unlike Elder Futhark's Uruz (aurochs), the Younger Futhark Úr shifted meaning to "drizzle" or "slag"—the dross from metalworking. The aurochs were extinct in Scandinavia by this time.

## Meaning

Rain, drizzle, and the byproduct of transformation. Slag is what remains when metal is purified—valuable ore leaves waste behind.

## In the Viking Age

The semantic shift reflects changing times. With no more wild aurochs, the rune's meaning adapted to the Viking world of rainy Northern skies and smithing.
