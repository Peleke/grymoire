Hagall is hail—destructive ice that falls without warning but melts to nourish. The Younger Futhark form differs visually from Elder Futhark's Hagalaz.

## Meaning

Sudden destruction, unavoidable natural force, and transformation through crisis. Hail destroys crops but brings water.

## In the Viking Age

The rune poems call hail "the coldest of grains"—acknowledging both its icy nature and its role in the cycle of destruction and renewal.
