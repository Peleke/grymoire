Ár means "plenty" or "good year"—related to Elder Futhark's Jera but focused specifically on agricultural abundance and prosperity.

## Meaning

Harvest, abundance, and the blessings of a good growing season. The reward for patience and hard work.

## In the Viking Age

Crucial in an agricultural society where one bad harvest meant starvation. Ár represented hope and gratitude.
