Ýr is the yew tree and the bow made from it—the final rune of Younger Futhark. Related to Elder Futhark's Eihwaz but transformed.

## Meaning

The yew's deadly beauty—evergreen in winter, poisonous, and the source of the finest bows. Death and endurance intertwined.

## In the Viking Age

Yew bows were prized weapons. The tree's ability to stay green through winter made it a symbol of life persisting through death.
