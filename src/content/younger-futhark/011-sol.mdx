Sól is the sun—life-giver, navigator's guide, and shield of the clouds. It continues Elder Futhark's Sowilo.

## Meaning

Light, guidance, hope, and victory. The sun was both practical navigation tool and symbol of triumph over darkness.

## In the Viking Age

Called "shield of the clouds" in the rune poems. For sailors, the sun meant life—the ability to navigate home.
