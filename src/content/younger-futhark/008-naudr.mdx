Nauðr is need, necessity, and constraint—continuing Elder Futhark's Nauthiz. Need breeds innovation; scarcity teaches value.

## Meaning

Hardship, lack, and the pressure that forces creativity. The need-fire kindled by friction when no other flame exists.

## In the Viking Age

Vikings knew need intimately—harsh winters, failed harvests, the ocean's indifference. Nauðr acknowledged this reality.
