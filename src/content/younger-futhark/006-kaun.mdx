Kaun means "ulcer" or "sore"—a significant shift from Elder Futhark's Kenaz (torch/knowledge). The meaning darkened in the Viking Age.

## Meaning

Illness, sores, and festering wounds. Disease was a constant companion in the medieval world, and this rune acknowledged its power.

## In the Viking Age

The Norwegian and Icelandic rune poems describe Kaun as "fatal to children" and "disease's increase"—unflinching reality.
