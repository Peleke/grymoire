Ís is ice—beautiful, treacherous, and temporary. It continues Elder Futhark's Isa but with Viking Age poetic elaboration.

## Meaning

Stillness, beauty, danger, and impermanence. Ice creates bridges across impassable waters but claims the unwary.

## In the Viking Age

The rune poems call ice "the broadest of bridges" and "blind man's bane"—acknowledging both its utility and peril.
