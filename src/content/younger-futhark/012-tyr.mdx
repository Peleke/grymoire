Týr names the god directly—the one-handed deity who sacrificed his hand to bind Fenrir. He represents law and martial honor.

## Meaning

Justice, sacrifice, martial honor, and keeping oaths even at great personal cost. Týr embodies the warrior who fights for right, not glory.

## In the Viking Age

Týr was invoked before battle. His rune was carved on weapons, asking for his blessing and his example of courage.
