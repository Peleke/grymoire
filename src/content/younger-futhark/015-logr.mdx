Lögr is water—lake, sea, and waterfall. It continues Elder Futhark's Laguz. For Vikings, water was road, livelihood, and grave.

## Meaning

Water in all forms, the sea's power, and the element that connected the Viking world. Water gives life and takes it.

## In the Viking Age

The sea was everything—trade route, raid path, fishing ground, and final resting place. Lögr acknowledged water's centrality.
