Fé is the Younger Futhark continuation of Elder Futhark's Fehu. In the Viking Age, it retained its meaning of wealth and cattle—the measure of a person's worth.

## Meaning

Wealth, prosperity, and material gain. The Vikings understood that gold hoarded loses its power—treasure must circulate through gift-giving and trade.

## In the Viking Age

Fé appears frequently in runic inscriptions related to inheritance, trade, and the commemoration of wealth accumulated in life.
