Sauil means "sun" — the great revealer.

The sun shows things as they are. Under its light, nothing hides. Shadow dissolves, color emerges, truth becomes visible. For peoples of the north, where winter darkness lasted months, the sun was not mere background but active blessing, fought for and celebrated.

The word survives in English "sun" and German "Sonne," stretching back to Proto-Indo-European roots. Humans have been naming this star since before they had writing, carrying the word across continents and millennia.

Numerically, Sauil carries 200. Two centuries. The completion of completion doubled. If 100 is the first fullness, 200 is that fullness confirmed and extended. This is not just achievement but established achievement, proven over time.

The sun teaches constancy. It rises every day. It does not take vacations or decide to skip a dawn. This reliability is what makes life possible — plants trust it, animals time their rhythms by it, civilizations build their calendars around it.

What in your life rises daily, reliably, without drama? What sun illuminates your world? And are you being as constant for others as the sun is for you?
