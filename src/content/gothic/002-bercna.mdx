Bercna means "birch" — one of the few Gothic letter names we can reconstruct with confidence.

The birch is a pioneer species. It colonizes disturbed ground, grows fast, dies young, and makes way for the forest that follows. It is transition made arboreal.

Numerically, Bercna carries the value 2. If Aza is unity, Bercna is the first split: self and other, yes and no, the original binary. Duality is where meaning begins — one thing can only be defined against what it is not.

The letter descends from Greek Beta, but the name is purely Germanic. Wulfila kept the Greek shape and grafted onto it a word from the forest. This is the Gothic method: adaptation without erasure.

In the birch, the Goths saw something worth naming. Fast growth. Pale bark that peels like parchment. A tree that bends in wind but rarely breaks.

Perhaps they saw themselves.
