Ezec carries the number 7 — perhaps the most significant number in the entire system.

Seven days of creation. Seven seals in Revelation. The number that recurs throughout Scripture, marking completion and divine order. For the Gothic Christians, 7 would have resonated with profound theological weight.

The letter represents the "z" sound, relatively rare in Gothic. It descends from Greek Zeta, maintaining both sound and position. The name "Ezec" is a reconstruction; like several Gothic letter names, certainty eludes us.

But the number is certain. 7. The number that ends the week and begins it again. The number of rest after labor, of fulfillment after striving. In Gothic numerology, to write 𐌶 was to invoke completion itself.

Consider: the week you're living through. Six days of making, doing, striving — and then the seventh, the pause that gives the others meaning. Without rest, labor is just motion. Without seven, six is just counting.

Ezec invites you to ask: what cycle are you completing? What rest have you earned?
