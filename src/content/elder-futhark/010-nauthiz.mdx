Nauthiz is the rune of need—specifically the "need-fire" kindled by friction when no other flame exists. Necessity drives invention; constraint breeds creativity.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to hardship, lack, and the lessons learned from scarcity. It represents the friction that creates fire—struggle that builds strength.

## In Divination

Nauthiz suggests constraints, delays, or unmet needs. It counsels patience and reminds us that current limitations may be forging future strengths.
