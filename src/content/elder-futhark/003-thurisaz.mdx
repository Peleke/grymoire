Thurisaz means "giant" or "thorn," connecting to both the Jötnar (giants) and to Thor, their eternal adversary. The thorn protects the rose; the giant tests the gods.

## Meaning

This is a rune of reactive force—protection that strikes back. It represents boundaries defended with sharp consequence, and the dangerous but necessary forces of chaos.

## In Divination

Thurisaz suggests a need for protection, a warning of conflict, or the presence of a powerful but potentially dangerous force. It counsels caution and strong boundaries.
