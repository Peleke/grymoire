Berkano is the birch tree—the first to leaf after winter, sacred to fertility goddesses. Its white bark was used to write upon, carrying messages and spells.

## Meaning

This rune governs birth, new beginnings, and nurturing growth. It represents the mother principle—not just biological motherhood, but all forms of patient cultivation.

## In Divination

Berkano suggests new beginnings, fertility, or nurturing situations. It may indicate pregnancy, new projects taking root, or the need for patient care.
