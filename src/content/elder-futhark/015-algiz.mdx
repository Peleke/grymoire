Algiz represents elk-sedge—a sharp marsh plant that cuts hands reaching for it—or perhaps elk antlers raised in warning. Either way: protection through sharp boundaries.

## Meaning

This rune is powerful protection, the guardian at the threshold. It represents sanctuary, divine protection, and boundaries that should not be crossed.

## In Divination

Algiz suggests protection is available or needed. It may indicate a guardian presence, the need to establish boundaries, or spiritual shielding.
