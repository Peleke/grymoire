Ehwaz represents the horse—not just transport, but sacred partner. The bond between horse and rider in Norse culture was one of deep trust and mutual reliance.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to partnership, trust, and cooperative movement. It represents progress achieved through teamwork, loyalty between allies.

## In Divination

Ehwaz suggests partnerships, travel, or progress through cooperation. It indicates loyal allies, trustworthy relationships, or vehicles of change.
