Perthro is perhaps the most mysterious rune—its exact meaning debated. Most scholars connect it to the lot cup used for casting runes or dice.

## Meaning

This rune governs fate, chance, mystery, and hidden things. It represents the unknown—not ignorance, but genuine mystery beyond human knowing.

## In Divination

Perthro suggests hidden influences, unexpected outcomes, or mysteries revealing themselves. It may indicate luck—good or ill—or secrets coming to light.
