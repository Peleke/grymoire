Ansuz is Odin's rune—the Allfather who hung nine nights on Yggdrasil to win the runes. It represents divine inspiration, the breath of life, and the power of the spoken word.

## Meaning

This rune governs communication, wisdom, and divine inspiration. It reminds us that words have power—to create, to heal, to curse. Odin gave humanity speech and consciousness.

## In Divination

Ansuz suggests messages incoming, the need for clear communication, or divine guidance arriving. Pay attention to signs, words, and your own inner voice.
