Raidho means "ride" or "journey"—both physical travel and the soul's journey through life. It represents movement in harmony with natural law and cosmic order.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to journeys both literal and metaphorical, to right action and proper timing. It suggests moving with the natural rhythm of things rather than against it.

## In Divination

Raidho often indicates travel, a journey beginning, or the need to take action. It can also suggest finding your proper path or aligning with natural cycles.
