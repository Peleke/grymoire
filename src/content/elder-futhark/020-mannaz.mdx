Mannaz means "human being"—representing humanity itself, the self, and our place within the human community. We are individuals within a greater whole.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to self-knowledge, human nature, and social bonds. It reminds us that we are both unique individuals and members of the human family.

## In Divination

Mannaz suggests self-reflection, human connections, or matters involving groups and society. It may indicate the need to know yourself or consider the human element.
