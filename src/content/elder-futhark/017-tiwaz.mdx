Tiwaz is the rune of Tyr, the god who sacrificed his hand to bind the wolf Fenrir. It begins the third aett—the aett of human experience.

## Meaning

This rune represents justice, victory in righteous battle, and sacrifice for the greater good. Tyr's courage came not from strength but from willingness to pay the price.

## In Divination

Tiwaz suggests victory through honor, legal matters resolved justly, or the need for sacrifice. It asks: what are you willing to give up to win?
