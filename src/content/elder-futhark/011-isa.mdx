Isa is ice—the simplest rune, a single vertical line. It represents absolute stillness, frozen potential, the pause between breaths.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to stasis, preservation, and the self. Ice preserves but also prevents growth. Sometimes stillness is necessary; sometimes it becomes a prison.

## In Divination

Isa suggests delays, frozen situations, or the need to pause. It may indicate ego issues or a situation that will not change until some thaw occurs.
