Othala closes the Elder Futhark with heritage—ancestral land, inherited wealth, and the wisdom passed down through generations. It is home in the deepest sense.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to ancestry, inherited traits and property, and belonging to a lineage. It represents what we receive from those who came before.

## In Divination

Othala suggests inheritance, family matters, or connection to ancestry. It may indicate property, tradition, or the need to honor where you come from.
