Fehu is the first rune of the Elder Futhark, representing cattle—the primary measure of wealth in ancient Germanic society. Unlike land, cattle represented mobile, shareable wealth.

## Meaning

Fehu speaks to abundance, prosperity, and the energy of wealth in motion. It reminds us that wealth must circulate to grow—hoarded riches stagnate and decay.

## In Divination

Upright, Fehu suggests incoming prosperity, success in ventures, and material gain. Reversed, it warns of loss, greed, or wealth poorly managed.
