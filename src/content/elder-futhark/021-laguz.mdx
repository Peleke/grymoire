Laguz is water in all its forms—lake, sea, river, rain. Water flows, reflects, conceals depths, and gives life. It represents the unconscious mind and intuition.

## Meaning

This rune governs flow, emotions, intuition, and the unconscious. Water finds its own level, takes the shape of its container, and always seeks the sea.

## In Divination

Laguz suggests following intuition, emotional matters, or things flowing naturally. It may indicate hidden depths, dreams, or the need to go with the flow.
