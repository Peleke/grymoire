Jera means "year" and represents the harvest—the reward that comes after a full cycle of planting, tending, and waiting. It cannot be rushed.

## Meaning

This rune governs cycles, seasons, and earned rewards. It reminds us that all things have their proper time and that patience yields abundance.

## In Divination

Jera promises rewards for effort, but in their proper season. It suggests good outcomes that require patience and continued work.
