Uruz represents the aurochs—the wild ox of ancient Europe, now extinct. Standing six feet tall at the shoulder, it embodied raw, untameable power.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to primal strength, physical health, and vital energy. It represents the wild, untamed forces within us—power that can be channeled but never fully controlled.

## In Divination

Uruz suggests a time of physical strength, healing, or the need to tap into your primal power. It may indicate necessary endings that lead to new strength.
