Sowilo is the sun—the ultimate source of light, life, and victory. It closes the second aett with the triumph of light over darkness.

## Meaning

This rune represents success, vitality, and wholeness. The sun guides travelers, nourishes crops, and defeats darkness. It is willpower made manifest.

## In Divination

Sowilo promises victory, success, and vital energy. It suggests clarity, guidance, and positive outcomes. The light shows the true path.
