Ingwaz represents the god Ing (Freyr)—lord of fertility, abundance, and the life force. Its shape suggests a seed or a pregnant moment of contained potential.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to fertility, internal growth, and potential energy waiting to manifest. It is the seed before sprouting—everything present, nothing yet visible.

## In Divination

Ingwaz suggests gestation, potential, or situations developing internally before manifesting. It counsels patience—the seed knows when to sprout.
