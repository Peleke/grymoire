Kenaz is the torch—controlled fire that illuminates, warms, and enables craft. It represents knowledge, creativity, and the inner fire of inspiration.

## Meaning

This rune governs learning, technical skill, and creative inspiration. It is fire tamed for human use—the spark of understanding that transforms raw material into art.

## In Divination

Kenaz suggests illumination, learning, or creative breakthroughs. It may indicate the need to apply knowledge practically or to kindle your inner fire.
