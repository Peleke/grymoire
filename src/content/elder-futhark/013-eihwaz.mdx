Eihwaz represents the yew tree—poisonous yet evergreen, associated with both death (yew bow, graveyard tree) and eternal life. It echoes Yggdrasil, the World Tree.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to endurance, transformation through death, and the axis connecting worlds. The yew stands in graveyards yet never loses its green.

## In Divination

Eihwaz suggests transformation, endings that enable new beginnings, or situations requiring endurance. It represents strength through all seasons.
