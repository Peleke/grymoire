Hagalaz begins the second aett with hail—destructive ice that falls from the sky, flattening crops before melting into nourishing water.

## Meaning

This rune represents unavoidable disruption, natural catastrophe that lies beyond human control. Yet within destruction lies the seed of transformation.

## In Divination

Hagalaz warns of disruption, sudden change, or forces beyond your control. It counsels acceptance of what cannot be changed and finding opportunity in crisis.
