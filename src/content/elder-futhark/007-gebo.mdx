Gebo means "gift" and represents the sacred bond created by giving and receiving. In Norse culture, gifts created obligations—a web of mutual support and alliance.

## Meaning

This rune speaks to generosity, partnership, and balanced exchange. The X shape suggests two forces meeting in equilibrium. A gift given freely creates connection.

## In Divination

Gebo suggests partnerships, contracts, or gifts incoming. It reminds us that relationships require reciprocity and that giving opens channels for receiving.
