Wunjo represents joy, harmony, and the deep satisfaction of wishes fulfilled. It is the bliss of fellowship—belonging to family, clan, and community.

## Meaning

This rune marks the end of the first aett (group of eight). It represents earned happiness, harmonious relationships, and alignment between desire and reality.

## In Divination

Wunjo suggests happiness, success, and harmony approaching. It indicates wishes coming true, joy in community, or a period of peace after struggle.
