Dagaz is daylight—specifically the liminal moment of dawn when darkness becomes light. Its shape suggests the balance point, the infinite moment of transformation.

## Meaning

This rune represents breakthrough, awakening, and total transformation. Unlike gradual change, Dagaz is sudden illumination—the light switch flipping.

## In Divination

Dagaz promises breakthroughs, enlightenment, and positive transformation. Night is ending. What was hidden becomes clear. A new day begins.
