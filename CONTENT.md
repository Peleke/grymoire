### Instagram Carousels: Gothic Words

Format:
```
Slide 1: Word in English (e.g., "father")
Slide 2: First letter glyph (𐌰)
Slide 3: Second letter glyph (𐍄)
Slide 4: Third letter glyph (𐍄)
Slide 5: Fourth letter glyph (𐌰)
Slide 6: Full word in Gothic script (𐌰𐍄𐍄𐌰)
```

Starter words:
- atta (father)
- aþþa (mother)
- guþ (god)
- hails (hello/hail)
- amen (yes, it's in Gothic)

Use Canva with parchment/ink aesthetic matching the site.

## Creation Workflow

### Capture (Mobile)
1. Draft content in Notion or Apple Notes while reading/studying
2. Include: word/rune/stanza, meaning, personal insight
3. Don't overthink - capture raw, refine later

### Process (Desktop, Weekly)
1. Review captured notes
2. Create MDX card for site (`src/content/[realm]/`)
3. Design IG carousel in Canva (if visual content)
4. Write caption / tweet text
5. Schedule posts (Buffer, Later, or native scheduling)

### Publish
1. Push MDX to repo → Vercel auto-deploys
2. Post to X + BlueSky (same content)
3. Post carousel to IG (when applicable)
4. Cross-post Reels to TikTok (Phase 2)
