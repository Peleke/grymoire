## Threads Forward

- **Tomis → Goths → Alaric:** The Ovid arc ends where the Alaric arc begins. Tomis is sacked c. 270 AD by the same people who produce Alaric. The Aurelian Walls go up at the same time.
- **Marlowe → Tamburlaine → Timurids → Peuce Island → Scythia Minor:** Marlowe's Amores translation opens the door to his Tamburlaine, which opens the Timurid renaissance, which connects to the geography of Ovid's exile.
- **Helga → Guðríður → Node 0:** The microfiction track feeds directly into the "Guðríður: The Heir" essay. Helga is the woman who stayed. Guðríður is the woman who left. The Ars Amatoria is the text that sits between them.
- **Interlinear → Latin platform:** Ars Amatoria III is the seed content. Nobody's first Latin text should be Caesar. It should be Ovid telling women how to dress.
- **Calligraphy → Roman Capitals → the full series:** Roman Capitals are historically correct for Ovid's era and visually striking. The daily practice filmed is content. The weekly quotes are Pinterest/IG. The hero pieces become site assets.
